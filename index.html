How do I make it ring? (last step)

iPhone with iOS 26 or newer (no GarageBand!)

  1. Tap Save to my iPhone and choose Save to Files.
  2. Open the Files app and tap your ringtone.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Use as Ringtone. (If you don't see it, tap More.)
  4. Done! Find it in Settings > Sounds & Haptics > Ringtone.

Don't see Use as Ringtone? Tap Save as .m4a instead above and try again with that file.

Older iPhone (iOS 25 or older)

  1. Save to Files, then open the free GarageBand app and start a new Audio Recorder song.
  2. Tap the loop button, then Files, then Browse items from the Files app. Pick your ringtone and drag it into the song.
  3. Tap the arrow, then My Songs. Press and hold your song, then Share, Ringtone, Export.
  4. Tap Use sound as… and choose Standard Ringtone.

Have a Mac? Plug in your iPhone, open Finder, and drag the .m4r file onto it.
